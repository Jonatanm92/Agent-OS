# 10. SEO metadata

Honest metadata only. No artist names, no ticketing-company names, no implied
affiliation, no keyword stuffing.

Set these in Shopify: product → *Search engine listing* → **Edit**.

## Product 1 — One Night

**Page title** (60 chars):
`Personalized Concert Memory Issue | Custom Keepsake`

**Meta description** (155 chars):
`Turn a concert into a personalized keepsake. Send your ticket, photos and story and we design a custom concert memory issue from the night. Digital, made to order.`

**URL handle:** `one-night-personalized-concert-memory-issue`

## Terms these target

Covered naturally by the copy, without stuffing: personalized concert gift,
concert memory gift, custom concert keepsake, concert photo keepsake, personalized
concert memories, concert anniversary gift, custom concert memory book.

Each appears at most once or twice across a page, in a sentence a person would
actually write. "Concert memory book" is deliberately used sparingly — the product
is currently digital, and the word "book" invites a print expectation the FAQ then
has to walk back.

## Rules that must not be broken

- **No artist or band names** in titles, descriptions, handles or alt text. It
  invites a trademark complaint and implies affiliation.
- **No ticketing-company names** (Ticketmaster, Live Nation, and so on).
- **No "official"**, "licensed", "authorised" or similar.
- Alt text on example images describes the *design* ("Cover page of a
  personalized concert issue"), never an artist or venue.

## Structured data

Shopify's themes emit Product structured data automatically. **Do not add review
or rating structured data** — there are no reviews. Marking up ratings that do
not exist is grounds for a Google manual action, on top of being false.

## Realistic expectation

These pages will not rank for anything competitive for months, and this store has
no domain authority yet. SEO is not the validation channel. It costs 10 minutes
to set honestly, and it makes the pages look right when a link is shared. Judge
the experiment on paid-order count, not on search traffic.
