## The one workflow

```text
FIND ──> HELP ──> CAPTURE ──> AUDIT ──> OFFER ──> TEST ──> PAY ──> DELIVER ──> PROOF ─┐
  ^                                                                                    │
  └────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

1. **Find:** search Threads daily for people hitting the problem right now.
2. **Help:** post one useful thing a day; answer their posts with something specific.
3. **Capture:** one ask — reply "audit".
4. **Audit:** a free check from 3 screenshots, back within 24 h. Give away the top fix.
5. **Offer:** only if the audit found exposure. One message, no chasing.
6. **Test:** lockdown + bot replay test on their staging site.
7. **Pay:** they pay after the test passes.
8. **Deliver:** go live with their approval; handover sheet.
9. **Proof:** with permission, post the before/after. It feeds step 1.

Urgency peaks *during* an attack, so step 1 matters most: someone posting "200 fake
orders this morning" today is the most likely first buyer.

## Readiness gates

**Gate A — free audit ready (by Day 9)**
- [ ] Audit checklist and one-page report template written
- [ ] Privacy line agreed: counts only, never customer names, emails or addresses
- [ ] Audit run once end to end on your own demo store

**Gate B — offer deliverable (by Day 15)**
- [ ] Bot replay test passes on your demo store: classic, block and Store API paths,
      including IP-rotating requests; real checkouts still succeed
- [ ] Express-pay coverage decided and tested
- [ ] Every commitment in the sales page's commitments table confirmed
- [ ] Payment link and simple terms (scope, access, liability) set up
- [ ] Sales page published (link in bio)

## Daily routine (~60 min)

| When | Task | Time |
|---|---|---|
| Morning | Post today's post | 5 min |
| First hour after posting | Reply to every comment on it | 10 min |
| Any time | **Find:** search Threads for `woocommerce`, `card testing`, `fake orders`, `failed orders`, `stripe declines`. Reply to 3 posts with something specific. No links unless asked. | 20 min |
| Any time | Audits and DMs: answer everyone who replied "audit" or DMed. Nobody else. | 15–25 min |
| End of day | Fill in the log row (bottom of this file) | 2 min |

**Weekly (Day 7, 14, 21, 28) — 30 min**
- Review the log; apply the decision rule for that week.
- Answer 1–2 card-testing threads on the WordPress.org support forums **for free, with no
  links or mention of the service** — the
  [forum guidelines](https://wordpress.org/support/guidelines/) ban promoting services.
  It sharpens the audit and teaches you the buyers' words.
- Check [jobs.wordpress.net](https://jobs.wordpress.net/) for checkout/fraud/bot jobs —
  that's where people post paid WordPress work, so responding there is fair game.

**Conversation rules:** reply only to people who asked; one follow-up after an audit, max;
never cold DM. Always give the top fix away free in the audit — if they can fix it
themselves, good. Trust is the asset you don't have yet.

## Decision rules (defaults — adjust)

| Day | If… | Then… |
|---|---|---|
| 7 | L1 got 0 "audit" replies and few views | Reach problem: double the Find replies (6/day), reply under bigger e-commerce accounts' posts |

| 14 | Gate B not met | Hold L3/L4; keep running audits; fix the blocker before anything else |
| 21 | Audits done, 0 yes | Ask each audited store: "What would have made this a yes?" Check whether their attack had already stopped (urgency gone) |
| 30 | ≥1 sale | Keep the plan; productize the audit as a read-only plugin; post L6-style proof monthly |
