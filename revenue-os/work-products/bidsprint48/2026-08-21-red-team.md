# BidSprint 48 — Revenue Red Team

Date: 2026-08-21

## Verdict

- **INTERNAL SOURCE-CONTROL PROOF: PASS**
- **CUSTOMER-GRADE DELIVERY PROOF: FAIL**
- **PAID OUTREACH READINESS: BLOCKED**

The historical dry run demonstrates source freezing, document/version ordering, application of later questions-and-answers, traceable requirement extraction, evidence-gap exposure and a defensible no-go. It does not prove a complete live customer package can be delivered within 48 hours at 1,900 SEK with acceptable legal, data-security and margin risk.

## What survived

1. The later Q&A deadline replaced the baseline tender deadline.
2. A stale ISO 27001 qualification requirement was not misclassified as active after the substantive requirement was removed.
3. The corrected maximum score was preserved.
4. Missing turnover, rating, certificate, consultant, price and authorization evidence remained `UNKNOWN` rather than being invented.
5. Tender-stage and contract-performance obligations were kept separate.
6. Prospecting remained human-gated; zero external messages were sent.

## Material failures

1. The 40-row matrix is a workflow demonstration, not a complete extraction of every mandatory technical line in the 157-page source package.
2. No controlled time log proves the full service fits the 48-hour promise or the owner-hour ceiling.
3. The 1,900 SEK price is not margin-proven after owner time, provider fees, corrections and delivery risk.
4. Seller identity, payment, binding customer terms, refund/cancellation wording, privacy, retention and AI/provider-chain gates remain owner-unverified.
5. The prospect queue proves reachability and tender relevance, not willingness to pay.
6. The historical central-government framework is close to the complexity ceiling for a low-priced founder pilot.

## Hard stops

Stop immediately if a material mandatory requirement lacks an exact source, source versions or Q&A completeness cannot be verified, excluded sensitive/security-protected material appears, the controlled full dry run exceeds eight owner hours without a reduction path, a customer discovers a material requirement/source error, or payment/contract handling would need to be improvised.

After deliverability is verified, kill or materially reposition the offer if thirty genuinely qualified, personally reviewed prospects produce zero payments and fewer than three serious buying signals.

## Minimum gates before first send

1. Complete one smaller in-scope customer-grade sample with every mandatory requirement and an independent second-pass audit.
2. Record owner time, agent time and rework by phase.
3. Freeze the exact scope, start condition, correction boundary and exclusions.
4. Owner-verify the legitimate seller/payment route and binding commercial wording.
5. Reverify a concrete procurement trigger and public recipient route for each first-batch prospect.
6. Stop for CEO approval of the exact first ten messages before any send.

## Decision

Continue internal preparation. Do not build SaaS. Do not contact prospects yet.
