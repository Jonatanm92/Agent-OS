# Founder Offer Red Team — Music Performance Release Pack

## Verdict
**TEST / REVISE — do not mark GO and do not build a full SaaS.**

The offer is sufficiently narrow to test and the production assets demonstrate that a coherent release pack can be made. It is not commercially validated: there is no recorded qualified-buyer payment, actual founder-fulfillment time is missing, the legitimate payment path is not live, and the remaining rights/source-credit record is incomplete.

## Strongest case against the offer

1. **The buyer may not value the distinction enough to pay.** Generic clippers, platform-native editors, templates and manual editing can appear “good enough.” Music-aware phrase selection and instrument framing must be experienced as a meaningful reduction in release work, not described as an AI feature.
2. **$29 may be operationally uneconomic.** Source inspection, downloading, phrase analysis, reframing, rendering, copy, QC, upload and one correction can consume more time than the price supports. No margin claim is credible until actual hands-on minutes and direct costs are logged for several orders.
3. **Source variability can destroy the promise.** Tight horizontal framing, hidden hands, low resolution, mismatched master audio, variable frame rate or poor sync can make two clean vertical clips impossible. A strict pre-payment source-fit check is mandatory.
4. **The offer can expand through revisions.** “Music-aware” is partly subjective. Without a written intake, objective acceptance test and one consolidated correction boundary, a $29 order can become open-ended creative editing.
5. **Rights and privacy can create disproportionate downside.** The customer must confirm authorization to supply files. Public use of a customer’s performance, name, quote or results requires separate permission. The service must not imply music-rights clearance or protection from platform claims.
6. **The proof may be founder-specific.** One technically strong metal-guitar example does not prove equal performance across drums, vocals, multicam footage, other genres or different filming styles. The first test segment should remain narrow enough to resemble the proof.
7. **Distribution is still manual and unproven.** A polished offer page and AI agents do not create demand. The test succeeds only through qualified conversations and real payments.

## Proof-specific defects that block GO
- Actual hands-on fulfillment minutes were not recorded.
- The release copy still contains an unresolved backing-audio/source placeholder.
- No explicit creator-supplied authorization record was found with the proof package.
- The current environment reverified codecs, hashes, full decoding and sampled framing, but did not independently perform a complete auditory phrase-boundary review of every clip.
- The legitimate international $29 checkout, receipt, intake handoff and refund path are not owner-verified.

## Narrowest defensible first segment
Independent guitarists and instrumental performance creators who:
- publish finished single-performer videos;
- have visible instrument and hand movement;
- already publish or intend to publish vertical content;
- can provide one stable, finished source file; and
- have direct buying authority.

Do not initially claim equal suitability for bands, complex multicam performances, music videos, talking-head education, automated high-volume clipping or full channel management.

## Cheapest remaining falsification sequence

### Test 0 — close operational gates
Before outreach:
1. record a complete auditory phrase-boundary check for the proof;
2. resolve exact source/credit wording and authorization record;
3. add three factual title options to the final delivery copy;
4. reconstruct or measure actual hands-on fulfillment time on the next full run; and
5. owner-verify the legitimate $29 payment, receipt, intake and refund path.

### Test 1 — first ten qualified prospects
Send only after CEO approval. Every prospect must score at least 8/10 and every message must cite one real performance.

Count as a credible buying signal only:
- a completed $29 payment;
- an explicit request to submit source for the paid pack;
- a serious price/scope discussion where the creator accepts the problem framing; or
- a request for a paid test rather than a free favor.

Do not count likes, views, compliments, generic curiosity, follows or “sounds cool.”

### Decision after ten
- **Continue:** at least one paid order, or at least two strong source-fit/price discussions with no repeated offer misunderstanding.
- **Revise message or offer:** qualified replies occur but buyers misunderstand the result, scope or why it differs from existing tools.
- **Investigate channel failure:** no response, but deliverability or contact-channel quality is uncertain.

### Kill threshold after thirty
Kill or materially reposition the offer when all of the following are true:
- thirty genuinely qualified prospects received personalized messages through legitimate public routes;
- message delivery and channel quality were checked;
- zero qualified buyers paid; and
- fewer than three prospects produced a serious buying signal.

Do not move the goalposts by changing audience, price, scope and channel simultaneously.

## Unit-economics gate
Track per order:
- source review minutes;
- download and organization minutes;
- musical selection minutes;
- edit and reframe minutes;
- rendering/wait time separately from active work;
- copy and packaging minutes;
- QC minutes;
- customer communication and correction minutes;
- payment fees, storage/transfer costs and refund loss.

At $29, the offer is not permitted to claim scalable margin until the measured active time and correction burden support it. If manual economics fail but buyers pay, raise price or automate only the proven bottleneck; do not expand scope.

## Required CEO decisions

- whether the test price remains $29;
- final turnaround and correction/review window;
- final refund/cancellation language for the selected payment route;
- permission to send the first ten exact messages; and
- permission for any public use of the founder proof under a separate brand.
