# Founder Intake and Delivery Package — Music Performance Release Pack

## Operating rule
Use this package only after the source has passed the preflight check. The founder pack is a bounded editing and release-packaging service; it is not recording, mixing, mastering, rights clearance, account management, or a promise of reach.

## 1. Pre-payment source-fit check
Ask the creator to provide either a private review link or a short representative sample before payment when practical.

Confirm all of the following:
- The file is a finished music performance in MP4 or MOV format.
- The performer, instrument and relevant hand movement are sufficiently visible for vertical reframing.
- Audio and picture appear synchronized and the file is not corrupt.
- The source has enough resolution and framing margin for the promised crops.
- The creator confirms they are authorized to supply the material for editing.
- The requested work fits the published founder scope.

Reply with one of three outcomes:
- **FIT:** source appears suitable; send the approved checkout link and intake form.
- **FIT WITH LIMITATION:** state the exact limitation before payment, such as reduced reframing options.
- **NOT A FIT:** explain the objective blocker; do not take payment for a pack that cannot be delivered.

## 4. Production checklist

### Preflight
- Verify file opens and decodes.
- Record source resolution, frame rate, duration and audio format.
- Check obvious sync, corruption, clipping and unusable framing.
- Confirm required facts and authorization are present.
- Create the order folder and immutable source copy or checksum.

### Selection and edit
- Identify candidate moments by musical phrase, not visual motion alone.
- Verify each selected clip begins and ends on a defensible musical boundary.
- Keep performer, instrument and relevant hand movement visible.
- Keep text outside the critical performance area.
- Preserve supplied finished audio unless a separately supplied master can be synchronized reliably.

### Export and packaging
- Export two vertical files in the agreed format.
- Produce one thumbnail/cover direction.
- Produce three factual title options, description, short copy and keyword/hashtag set.
- Use unambiguous file names.
- Add a manifest listing each deliverable, dimensions, duration and checksum.
- Record actual hands-on minutes by phase.
- Run the written founder-proof acceptance test before delivery.

## 7. Acceptance request

Use a single explicit response:

> Please reply with either **ACCEPTED** or one consolidated correction list tied to the original intake. Acceptance confirms that the delivered files open correctly and that you have checked the factual copy, crop, synchronization and required credits. It does not waive rights provided by applicable law or any separately approved refund terms.

Record the response and acceptance date in Revenue OS. Do not treat silence as a testimonial or permission to publish the customer’s work.

## 8. Correction and support boundary

Included correction:
- factual spelling or link error against supplied information;
- export or playback defect;
- demonstrable audio/video sync defect;
- critical crop error against the agreed framing requirement; or
- omitted deliverable listed in the order confirmation.

Outside the included correction:
- replacement video or audio;
- a new selected section after compliant clips were delivered;
- a new visual identity or creative direction;
- mixing, mastering, restoration, stem work or recording changes;
- extra clips, alternate aspect ratios or posting into accounts; and
- rights-clearance work or a guarantee against platform claims.

Do not improvise refund or cancellation promises. Use only the final CEO-approved wording for the selected legitimate payment route.

## 9. Testimonial and referral request

Ask only after explicit acceptance:

> Thanks for confirming the pack. What part of the release workflow did this remove for you, and what nearly stopped it from being useful? A one- or two-sentence answer is enough. May I quote your answer publicly with your approved name/handle and link? “No” is completely fine.

A separate, explicit “yes” is required before using the customer’s name, quote, files or results publicly. A referral request may be made once; do not create a repeated follow-up sequence.
