# 30-Prospect Qualification Rubric

## Objective
Find reachable independent music-performance creators who can plausibly buy the $29 founder workflow. Do not optimize for follower count alone.

## Mandatory inclusion criteria
A prospect must satisfy all of the following:
1. Publishes finished instrumental or music-performance video, not mainly talking-head content.
2. Has published at least one relevant long-form performance in the last 120 days, or at least three in the last 12 months.
3. Uses at least one short-form platform, or has an obvious repurposing gap that the offer can solve.
4. Has a legitimate public contact route intended for creator/business communication.
5. Is independent enough that a direct founder offer is plausible; large label or agency procurement is not required.
6. The public content gives enough specific context to write a genuinely personalized message without inventing facts.

## Exclude
- Inactive accounts with no recent release behaviour.
- Speech-first creators for whom generic clippers are the obvious solution.
- Accounts where contact would require bypassing privacy controls or scraping non-public data.
- Creators whose content cannot reasonably be edited into the promised output.
- Agencies, labels, or enterprise teams requiring procurement for a $29 test.
- Prospects already receiving consistently strong, music-aware release packaging from a team.

## Evidence fields to record
- Creator name / public handle
- Public contact route
- Country / language when publicly clear
- Most relevant recent performance and publication date
- Long-form platform
- Short-form platform and recent activity
- Observable repurposing gap
- One specific, factual personalization hook
- Qualification score
- Contact status and outcome
- Source links retained internally

## 10-point qualification score
- 0–2: recent long-form performance frequency
- 0–2: active short-form use or visible repurposing gap
- 0–2: direct public contactability
- 0–2: obvious fit for music-aware selection/framing/packaging
- 0–1: independent buying authority appears plausible
- 0–1: enough public detail for honest personalization

### Priority
- 8–10: first outreach batch
- 6–7: reserve / second batch
- 0–5: exclude

## Batch quality gate
The first batch may not be sent unless all ten prospects score at least 8/10, each message references a real public performance, and the CEO approves the final brand, offer, and exact messages.

## Validation thresholds
- Continue testing when qualified prospects engage in serious problem/price discussion, request a sample, book a call, or pay.
- Rework the offer after the first ten qualified prospects if messages are read/replied to but the value proposition is misunderstood.
- Kill or materially reposition after 30 qualified, personalized contacts produce no credible buying signal, subject to message deliverability and channel quality being verified.
