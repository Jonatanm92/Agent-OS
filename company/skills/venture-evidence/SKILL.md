# Venture Evidence

Use this skill before committing production build effort to a product, service, automation, media property, or acquisition channel.

## Inputs

- proposed buyer and painful job
- offer and price hypothesis
- proposed acquisition route
- known evidence and source artifacts
- founder constraints and delivery boundary

## Method

1. Restate the thesis as: buyer + triggering event + painful job + current workaround + proposed outcome + price + acquisition route.
2. Create an evidence ledger. For each claim record source, date, observed/sourced/inferred/unknown class, independence, contradiction, and implication.
3. Score using these bounded dimensions:
   - pain urgency: 0–15
   - willingness to pay: 0–15
   - reachability: 0–12
   - proof speed: 0–10
   - delivery feasibility: 0–10
   - gross margin: 0–10
   - recurring potential: 0–8
   - differentiation: 0–8
   - founder fit: 0–7
   - evidence quality: 0–5
   - risk penalty: subtract 0–20
4. Count only traceable independent evidence.
5. Identify the strongest falsification condition.
6. Design the smallest experiment that can materially update the decision.

## Production evidence gate

A commercial venture is not build-ready until all are true:

- score is at least 75/100 after risk penalty
- at least 10 independent pain signals
- at least 3 price or payment signals
- at least 20 reachable qualified prospects
- technical feasibility probe passed with recorded output
- acquisition route documented with a measurable response event
- privacy, legal, security, and delivery-risk review complete

## Decision

- below 65: `KILL`
- 65 or above with missing evidence: `EXPERIMENT`
- 75 or above with complete evidence: `BUILD-READY`
- 85 or above with complete evidence: `PRIORITY`

## Output

Return: thesis, score breakdown, evidence ledger, contradictions, decision, production-build permission, missing evidence, falsification test, next experiment, cost ceiling, owner gate, and expected information gain.
