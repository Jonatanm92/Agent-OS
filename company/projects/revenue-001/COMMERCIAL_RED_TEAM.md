# Commercial Red Team — Revenue Mission 001

**Date:** 2026-08-19  
**Verdict:** EXPERIMENT / DO NOT SCALE  
**Score:** 61/100 before direct-market evidence

## What survives attack

- The demo can be built safely and cheaply with no external model or integration.
- The offer is bounded to one workflow, one correction round, synthetic/redacted input, and mandatory human approval.
- Public websites expose a reachable acquisition route and structured inquiry forms.
- A 6,900 SEK founder price is low enough to test without pretending to be enterprise software.

## What is not proven

1. **Pain:** a form or inbox does not prove costly friction. Current direct pain signals: 0.
2. **Payment:** no buyer has accepted the price or offered a budget. Current price/payment signals: 0.
3. **Volume:** small firms may receive too few inquiries for the problem to matter.
4. **Existing substitutes:** CRM templates, office staff, or automation already in place may be adequate.
5. **Delivery economics:** 6,900 SEK becomes unprofitable if integrations, custom categories, data cleanup, or ongoing support enter scope.
6. **Trust:** “AI automation” may reduce confidence in a customer-facing workflow. Lead with review-ready intake and human control.
7. **Data/security:** real emails may contain personal data, access codes, addresses, photos, insurance facts, or safety-critical details.
8. **Emergency risk:** urgency classification cannot be allowed to delay emergency response or create a false diagnosis.
9. **Acquisition legality/reputation:** careless automated outreach would create compliance and brand risk.
10. **Founder capacity:** discovery, delivery, correction, and support must fit around the owner’s full-time work.

## Required controls

- sell the outcome, not autonomous AI;
- keep founder validation synthetic/redacted;
- no production connector or automatic sending at 6,900 SEK;
- written acceptance criteria and customer responsibilities;
- one correction round and a hard out-of-scope boundary;
- no emergency dispatch/diagnosis claim;
- legal-person generic channels only for the first test;
- source/suppression ledger and owner-reviewed messages;
- Frilans Finans/contracting route established before work is agreed or begins;
- separate production price and data/security decision after evidence.

## Cheapest decisive experiment

1. Complete current qualification for ten candidates.
2. Owner approves one message, one follow-up, ten recipients, zero ad spend, and a 14-day measurement window.
3. Record qualified replies and run up to three 20-minute discovery conversations.
4. Show the synthetic demo only after the prospect confirms the workflow exists.
5. Ask a concrete payment question near 6,900 SEK; do not count compliments as payment evidence.

## Pass threshold

Continue only when the first controlled batch produces at least:

- 3 qualified replies or conversations;
- 3 recurring-pain confirmations with a concrete recent example;
- 2 explicit price/payment signals;
- 1 prospect willing to define a bounded paid pilot, subject to contracting readiness.

## Stop/revise threshold

Pause when ten verified prospects yield no qualified reply, three conversations reject the problem, the expected workflow cannot be delivered inside the fixed boundary, or a legal/security issue cannot be contained. Do not compensate by building more features.

## Final recommendation

Approve completion of internal assets and a **bounded validation gate**, not general autonomous selling, production deployment, or a claim of income. The first economic milestone is a buyer-approved paid pilot—not dashboard activity, simulated revenue, or a verbal expression of interest.
