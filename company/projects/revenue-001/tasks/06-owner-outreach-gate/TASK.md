This is a board/owner gate, not an autonomous agent task.

## Evidence required before the decision

- founder offer and scope frozen
- Commercial Red Team verdict recorded
- synthetic demonstration approved by QA / Security
- 30-prospect evidence pack reviewed
- outreach drafts and discovery process complete
- measurement and stop rules defined
- Frilans Finans readiness checklist complete
- privacy, legal, security, and delivery-risk review complete
- expected model/tool cost and owner time visible

## Owner decision options

- **APPROVE CONTROLLED OUTREACH:** authorize only the reviewed messages, prospect set, channel, volume, and measurement window.
- **REVISE:** return named gaps to the responsible role.
- **KILL:** stop the wedge and preserve the evidence for portfolio review.

## After approval

Revenue Operations may prepare the approved next actions, but the owner remains the sender unless a later explicit automation permission is granted. Any prospect reply, price objection, call, proposal, contract, account action, or invoice submission creates a new recorded owner gate.

## Stop rules

Pause and return to Commercial Red Team when response quality is materially below the approved threshold, buyers reject the problem or price, the delivery boundary expands, a legal/security issue appears, or the expected pilot cannot be delivered within the approved cost and support ceiling.
