# Founder offer — Förfrågan till arbetsklart underlag

**Version:** 1.0 internal founder-validation contract  
**Date:** 2026-08-19  
**Status:** Frozen for validation; no external promise has been made

## One-sentence promise

For a Swedish installation or field-service company with a manual inquiry inbox, we turn one bounded inquiry workflow into clearer priorities, fewer missing-information loops, and review-ready response/task drafts — while a human retains every external decision.

## Buyer, problem, outcome

- **Economic buyer:** owner, operations manager, service manager, or office manager in an active Swedish AB.
- **Primary profile:** approximately 5–49 employees; VVS, electrical, ventilation, heat-pump, solar, lock/port, relining, sheet-metal, or comparable field service.
- **Observed starting point:** quote/service inquiries arrive through a generic email address, website form, or phone-to-office handoff.
- **Problem hypothesis:** incoming inquiries vary in completeness and urgency, creating repeated clarification, inconsistent prioritization, and manual transfer into internal work.
- **Outcome hypothesis:** a transparent intake workflow gives staff a consistent first assessment, questions to ask, a draft response, an internal job card, and a follow-up date.

This is a hypothesis until direct buyer conversations and payment evidence exist.

## Founder pilot: 6,900 SEK excluding VAT

Delivery begins only after the contracting/payment route is valid and the input boundary is accepted.

### Included

1. One 45-minute workflow-mapping session.
2. One intake route: generic email export, form export, spreadsheet, or manually supplied redacted examples.
3. Up to 25 synthetic or customer-redacted historical inquiry examples.
4. One language: Swedish.
5. A bounded workflow producing:
   - category and visible confidence/source fields;
   - urgency flag requiring human confirmation;
   - missing-information checklist;
   - response draft that is not sent automatically;
   - internal task/job-card draft;
   - follow-up date;
   - transition/audit record;
   - mandatory human approval before external action.
6. A local or isolated demonstration, acceptance report, and 30-minute handover.
7. One correction round for defects against the written acceptance criteria, requested within seven calendar days.

### Explicitly excluded

- production deployment, continuous hosting, SLA, on-call support, or 24/7 operation;
- automatic email/SMS sending, booking, quoting, purchasing, payment, or CRM writes;
- customer credentials, live mailbox access, or unrestricted personal data;
- custom CRM/ERP/accounting integration;
- model fine-tuning, broad chatbot functionality, voice agent, or mobile application;
- legal, regulatory, tax, or cybersecurity certification;
- guaranteed revenue, time savings, accuracy, lead conversion, or head-count reduction.

### Customer responsibilities

- identify one accountable process owner;
- provide synthetic/redacted examples and explain the current workflow;
- verify categories, rules, wording, and urgency thresholds;
- keep real credentials and unredacted personal data out of the founder pilot;
- review and approve every output before any use;
- attend mapping and handover sessions.

## Delivery sequence

1. Contract/payment route and data boundary confirmed.
2. Current workflow mapped and acceptance criteria signed off.
3. Synthetic/redacted fixture set created.
4. Isolated demonstration configured.
5. Deterministic tests and security/privacy self-check run.
6. Buyer walkthrough and acceptance check.
7. One bounded correction round, then delivery closes.

Target delivery hypothesis: **five working days after complete input and approvals**. This is not promised until delivery capacity is checked for the specific assignment.

## Acceptance criteria — synthetic demonstration

The demonstration must:

- process the agreed fixture set deterministically;
- expose classification, confidence, and source fields;
- detect agreed required-information gaps without inventing values;
- draft but never send a response;
- create a visible internal task and follow-up date;
- block external action until explicit human approval;
- show a transition log and a safe failure path;
- pass the documented tests/build;
- contain no customer credentials or production integration;
- be removable without remote-resource cleanup.

## Future production-pilot gate

A separate production pilot can be proposed only after the founder pilot demonstrates real buyer value and the parties agree on data processing, security, integrations, support, and acceptance. Minimum evidence:

- at least two explicit price/payment signals;
- one customer workflow owner confirms the output would change a real process;
- approved data map and lawful basis;
- named human approver and rollback process;
- technical probe against the actual integration route;
- priced support/maintenance boundary.

## Pricing hypothesis after validation

- **Founder validation:** 6,900 SEK excluding VAT.
- **Regular implementation hypothesis:** 14,900–29,000 SEK excluding VAT for a bounded production pilot, depending on integration and data risk.
- **Optional support hypothesis:** 1,900–4,900 SEK/month only after a real support burden is measured.

No higher price is justified until buyers accept the problem, at least two price signals exist, and delivery effort is measured. Out-of-scope work requires a written quote; internal planning assumption is 1,250 SEK/hour excluding VAT, not yet a market promise.

## Privacy and security boundary

- Synthetic/redacted data only in founder validation.
- No secrets in prompts, repository, logs, screenshots, or outreach assets.
- No production mailbox/CRM access.
- No automatic external action.
- No use for emergency dispatch, safety-critical diagnosis, regulated professional decisions, employee surveillance, credit decisions, or sensitive-person profiling.
- Real personal data requires a separate data map, lawful-basis review, processor/recipient review, retention rules, access controls, incident route, and appropriate agreement before processing.

## Qualification

A company qualifies for discovery when it is an active Swedish legal person, roughly 5–49 employees, has a visible manual inquiry route, sells field-service/installation work, and can name a workflow owner. It must be possible to test one bounded process without production access.

## Disqualification

Disqualify or defer when the firm is inactive/insolvent, below/above the operating range without a strong exception, has no observable inquiry route, needs regulated or safety-critical automation, demands automatic sending in the founder scope, cannot supply redacted/synthetic examples, or expects broad integration within 6,900 SEK.

## Strongest commercial counterargument

The buyer may have low inquiry volume, already use adequate CRM templates, or view the workflow as a minor administrative inconvenience rather than a paid problem. The solution can become expensive custom consulting disguised as automation.

## Falsification and stop rule

Revise or kill the wedge when a controlled ten-prospect test produces no qualified replies, three discovery conversations fail to confirm recurring intake friction, or no buyer provides an explicit price/payment signal near 6,900 SEK. Do not respond by adding features before retesting the problem and acquisition route.

## Commercial Red Team verdict

**EXPERIMENT — not a production build.** The scope is narrow enough to test, the demo can be safe and cheap, and the public inquiry routes make acquisition testable. Pain and willingness to pay remain unproven.
