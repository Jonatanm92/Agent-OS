# 30-prospect evidence ledger

**Capture date:** 2026-08-19  
**External contact:** none  
**Purpose:** internal founder validation, not a mailing list

The CSV contains 30 unique Swedish installation/field-service candidates and a public source for the observable inquiry route. It deliberately separates **observable facts** from the **pain/buying hypothesis**. Public forms prove reachability and workflow structure; they do not prove that the company experiences a painful problem or will pay.

This means the **30-row ledger exists**, but the task is not allowed to claim 30 fully qualified prospects yet. That is an evidence-integrity decision, not a missing-data concealment.

## Observable workflow signals

Across the ledger, public sites expose recurring patterns that the demonstration can address:

- free-quote forms with free-text descriptions;
- separate service/product categories;
- promises of fast response;
- emergency versus normal service requests;
- support forms for existing versus new customers;
- missing technical/property information likely to require follow-up;
- central generic inboxes that must route work internally.

These are **workflow signals**, not ten independent pain confirmations. Direct pain evidence must come from qualified conversations, lost-time examples, complaint/review evidence, or existing-process measurements.

## Substitute and competitor map

The buyer can solve the job with:

- existing CRM/helpdesk forms and templates;
- Microsoft 365/Google Workspace rules;
- Zapier, Make, Power Automate, or n8n;
- vertical field-service systems;
- a shared mailbox plus office coordinator;
- generic AI assistants or custom consultants;
- doing nothing because inquiry volume is low.

The founder offer must therefore win on bounded implementation, transparency, human approval, local/synthetic demonstration, and speed to a measured decision—not on generic “AI automation”.

## Acquisition hypothesis

A low-volume, owner-reviewed B2B test through generic company inboxes/forms can validate whether the public workflow signal maps to a paid problem. Initial metric window:

- 10 verified legal-person prospects;
- one first message and at most one follow-up;
- primary metric: qualified positive replies;
- secondary: discovery calls, recurring-pain confirmations, explicit price/payment signals;
- stop/revise when no qualified reply appears after the approved batch or buyers consistently reject problem/price.

## Privacy and compliance notes

- Use generic company channels; do not target sole traders or private addresses in the first test.
- Store only company name, source, role hypothesis, and public generic route.
- Record source date, purpose, suppression/objection status, and deletion/review date.
- Every message must identify the sender, state the business relevance, and provide an easy objection/stop route.
- Do not automate sending. The owner remains sender unless a later explicit permission is recorded.
- This operating note is not a legal opinion; final outreach must follow current Swedish/EU rules and the owner’s approved risk posture.
