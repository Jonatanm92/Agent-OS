Prepare the internal evidence base for founder validation. Do not contact prospects.

## Qualification

A prospect must be a Swedish installation or field-service firm with roughly 5–49 employees, a visible route to a decision-maker, and evidence that quote or service inquiries arrive through a web form, email, or comparable manual intake.

## Required output

- 30 unique qualified firms
- public source and capture date for each qualification claim
- decision-maker role and public contact route where lawfully visible
- observed workflow signal and why the offer may be relevant
- personalization fact that can be verified publicly
- disqualifiers and data-quality warning
- 10 independent pain signals across credible sources or future direct conversations
- substitute/competitor map
- acquisition-channel hypothesis and response metric
- privacy and outreach-compliance notes

## Evidence integrity

Do not infer employee count, pain, software stack, email address, or buying intent without marking it as an inference. Do not include private or sensitive personal data. Duplicates and generic directory entries do not count as qualified prospects.

## Acceptance evidence

The evidence ledger contains 30 qualified rows with sources, contradictions, and confidence. Commercial Red Team reviews the list before any outreach draft is approved.
