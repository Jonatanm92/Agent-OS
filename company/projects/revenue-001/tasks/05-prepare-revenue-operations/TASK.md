Prepare every internal commercial artifact needed for the owner to begin founder validation after approval.

## Required artifacts

- one-page founder offer and FAQ
- concise discovery script and qualification checklist
- personalized outreach drafts mapped to the 30-prospect evidence pack
- objection and response library
- CRM stage definitions with evidence required to advance
- measurement sheet for sends, replies, substantive conversations, pain signals, price signals, proposals, paid pilots, delivery outcome, and support time
- founder-pilot scope and change-control language
- Frilans Finans readiness checklist: account status, assignment registration before compensation is agreed, customer/invoice fields, VAT/tax assumptions to verify, payment terms, and owner-only actions
- fallback process when a customer requires a supplier form, data-processing agreement, or security questionnaire

## Restrictions

Do not send messages, submit invoices, accept contracts or platform terms, create binding commitments, change bank/payment details, or claim that Frilans Finans has approved a specific assignment.

## Acceptance evidence

All artifacts are internally complete, every legal/tax/payment assumption is marked for verification, and the owner can see exactly which actions require identity, BankID/2FA, account approval, or a customer conversation.
