# Objective

Obtain enough real commercial and technical evidence to decide whether to sell the first founder pilot at **6,900 SEK excluding VAT**, revise the offer, or kill the wedge.

# Initial buyer

Swedish installation and field-service firms with 5–49 employees that receive quote or service inquiries through website forms or email and perform repetitive classification, missing-information follow-up, internal task creation, and reminders.

# Workflow hypothesis

Inquiry arrives → classify intent → detect missing information → draft a response → create an internal task → human approves any external message → schedule follow-up reminder.

# Validation targets

- 30 qualified reachable prospects
- 10 independent pain signals
- 3 price or payment signals
- 5 substantive buyer conversations or equivalent written exchanges
- 1 paid founder pilot
- technical probe passed using synthetic data
- acquisition route and response metric documented
- privacy, legal, security, and delivery-risk review completed

Targets are not facts. Every completed target requires a source artifact.

# Scope boundary

Before the owner approves external contact, the project may create only internal research, synthetic demonstrations, qualification records, offer material, discovery questions, outreach drafts, acceptance contracts, risk reviews, and payment-readiness checklists.
