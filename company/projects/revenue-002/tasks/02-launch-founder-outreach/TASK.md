Verify one decision maker and one public business contact route for each account in `PROSPECTS.md`. Create one 45–60 second target-specific demonstration and one short message per company.

The call to action is a 10-minute walkthrough. Do not lead with technical architecture, “AI transformation,” or a long feature list. Do not accuse the company of losing leads; show a useful workflow based on the inbound channels it already publishes.

After the owner approves the exact recipients and messages, send through the approved business channel and record:

- sent date and channel
- reply status
- next follow-up date
- demo booked or declined
- stated objection or need

No mass outreach and no more than one follow-up without a response.
