# Objective

Create the first **real accepted founder pilot** for LeadVakt. The operating order is now:

**personal observation → working demo → direct conversation → accepted pilot → delivery → payment**

Research and red-team work may improve execution, but they may not block the first five personalized contacts once the demo and messages are accurate.

# Buyer

Owner-led Swedish VVS, heat-pump, cooling, electrical, or field-service companies that receive customer inquiries through telephone, SMS, email, or website forms and can lose work when the first response is delayed or incomplete.

Initial geography: Vänersborg, Trollhättan, Vargön, Uddevalla, and nearby Västra Götaland.

# Product

**LeadVakt** is a narrow inbound lead-response workflow:

1. receive or register an unanswered inquiry
2. classify service type and urgency
3. detect missing information
4. prepare an immediate first-response draft
5. ask only the required qualification questions
6. create the next internal action
7. hand the lead to a human for quotation, booking, or technical judgment
8. record response time and outcome

The founder version does not promise a full autonomous telephone receptionist. Missed-call SMS, email, and website-form workflows are the first implementation paths. Voice may be added only when call volume and economics justify it.

# Founder offer

- **2,900 SEK excluding VAT**
- installation of one agreed inbound workflow
- 30 days of use and correction support
- no binding period
- optional continuation: **990 SEK excluding VAT per month**
- payment requested only after the agreed installation has been demonstrated and accepted

# Included

- one inbound source or manual missed-lead intake
- immediate acknowledgement template
- classification and urgency rules
- missing-information questions
- human handoff and follow-up queue
- a simple result view
- installation notes and 30-day correction period

# Excluded

- autonomous quotation or binding booking
- broad CRM replacement
- custom ERP integration
- unrestricted outbound messaging
- full voice-agent call handling
- fabricated revenue or customer-result claims

# Commercial scoreboard

Only these count as progress:

- personalized contacts actually approved and sent
- substantive replies
- demos booked
- written pilot acceptance
- invoices/payment requests issued through an approved legal route
- verified money received

Internal scorecards, simulations, generated leads, and model output count as **0 SEK revenue**.

# Immediate execution rule

The first five target accounts have already been identified in `PROSPECTS.md`. Each receives one concrete observation and one short demo angle. There is no requirement to collect 30 prospects or 10 pain signals before contact.

The only remaining owner gate is approval of the exact external messages and any binding commercial/payment action.
