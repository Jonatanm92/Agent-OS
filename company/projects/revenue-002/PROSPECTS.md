## Qualification notes

These companies are candidates, not claimed customers. A public form or telephone number does not prove that they currently lose leads. The first contact must therefore be framed as a short personalized demonstration, not as an accusation that their process is broken.

## Contact order

1. Verify owner/contact person and current services.
2. Use the company-specific angle above to create a 45–60 second screen recording.
3. Send one short message asking whether a 10-minute walkthrough is relevant.
4. Follow up once after two business days.
5. Stop after a clear no or no response to the single follow-up.
